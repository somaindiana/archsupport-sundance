Meet Aklilu
“Aki” Gebrie

Aki is an architectural designer based in Budapest, with an MSc in Architecture from Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME). His work for Sundance includes the design of MiniRaktar’s Phase I layout, alongside working drawings and space-planning packages. He also provides ongoing remote support to a UK residential practice.

You brief Aki directly and review the work with him. His experience includes residential remodels, new-build housing layouts and large-scale self-storage planning. He speaks fluent English and works UK hours.
